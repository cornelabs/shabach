Overtime Penalty Rule: Performances that run past their category's designated time limit receive an automatic 2-point deduction for each completed overtime interval. Durations are officially recorded by the score desk stopwatches and verified before collation locking.
4. The 100-Point Cake Adjudication Model
95% — Independent Judges Panel
Four appointed judges evaluate every finalist on a 5-criterion rubric:
  • Musicality / Technical Execution (20 pts)
  • Biblical Depth & Message (20 pts)
  • Originality & Creativity (20 pts)
  • Stage Craft & Presentation (20 pts)
  • Audience Engagement & Ministry (15 pts)
Subtotal out of 95. The four judges' raw subtotals are averaged.
5% — Verified Public Audience Vote
Audience members vote via email OTP verification (1 vote per person).
  • Top vote-getter earns the full 5.0 points
  • Remaining acts receive points scaled proportionally
  • Votes cannot be cancelled or changed once recorded
Final Score = (Judges Average / 95 * 95) + Audience Points - Overtime Penalties.
5. Officials Operational Protocol & Integrity Safeguards
For Judges
1. Navigate to /judges and enter your 4-digit PIN.
2. Score each criterion (0 to max) as acts perform.
3. Save drafts if needed. Verify all 5 criteria before clicking Submit.
4. Declare conflicts of interest if scoring acts from your own district.
For Score Desk (Admin)
1. Unlock /organiser with ADMIN2026.
2. Enter stopwatch durations for all 16 acts.
3. Verify all 64 scorecards (4 judges x 16 acts) are submitted.
4. Confirm tie-breaks and score desk review.
5. Lock results to publish podium standings to /results.
For Technical & Ushers
1. Direct audience to /contestants to vote.
2. Advise voters to enter their email and verify the 6-digit code sent to inbox.
3. Remind voters that every person receives only 1 vote across all acts.
4. In case of issues, contact the Chief Score Desk.
Six Mandatory Integrity Gates before Result Publication:
① Voting Closed & Verified  •  ② 64 Submitted Scorecards  •  ③ Durations Logged & Audited  •  ④ Conflict Declarations Resolved  •  ⑤ Deterministic Tie-Breaks Verified  •  ⑥ Executive Score Desk Dual-Lock Confirmation.
