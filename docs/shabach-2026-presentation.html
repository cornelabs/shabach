How fans vote quickly and safely on their smartphones in 3 simple steps.

Scan or Search
Scan contestant QR code on stage screens or search by name/code on shabach.vercel.app.

Enter Email
Type email address. System sends a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) immediately.

Voting is CLOSED until Act 16 finishes. The director opens it for 15 minutes, then closes it permanently.

2. The 4 Judges' Workspace

Judges score 16 acts on dedicated iPads/tablets at /judges/judge1 through judge4.

5 Category Criteria

Specific Rubrics

Music, Spoken Word, Drama, and Dance have tailored criteria (each out of 20, plus 15 for Audience Impact = 95 max).

Live Safety Indicator

If a judge coached or mentors an act, they tick "I declare a conflict of interest". The card is flagged for score-desk resolution.

Entity Deep-Dive · Time Enforcement

3. The Timekeeper & Overtime Penalty

Fairness means keeping performances strictly within their allocated duration.

⏱️ Category Time Limits

• Music: 4 minutes (240 seconds)

• Dance: 4 minutes (240 seconds)

• Drama: 5 minutes (300 seconds)

• Spoken Word: 3 minutes 30 seconds (210 seconds)

📏 The Overtime Rule

−2 Points per 30 Seconds

If an act exceeds their limit by 30 full seconds, 2 points are deducted automatically by the software.

Example: A Music act finishes in 4:35 (35s over limit). They incur 1 penalty interval = −2 points.

1
First Note / Step
Timekeeper starts the digital stopwatch on the very first sound or movement.
2
Final Bow / Cut
Stopwatch stops on the final note. Elapsed time is written on a physical slip.
3
Score Desk Entry
Officer enters mm:ss on /organiser. Penalty calculates instantly with zero math mistakes.
🎙️ Presenter Script: "The timekeeper uses a physical stopwatch. When the act ends, they hand the slip to the score desk. The system calculates the deduction automatically: -2 points for every full 30 seconds over limit. No manual math, no arguments."

Edge Cases · Human Error Handling

What If a Judge Makes a Clerical Mistake?

A judge entered 2 instead of 20 by accident? Here is how our audit trail handles it safely.

1
Judge Notifies Chief Judge
The adjudicator immediately flags the clerical typo to the Chief Judge and Score Desk.
2
Score Desk Reopens Card
On /organiser, the officer clicks the Reopen button next to that specific judge's row.
3
Mandatory Audit Reason
The system requires a written explanation (e.g. "Clerical typo approved by Chief Judge"). Blank reasons are rejected.
4
Judge Resubmits
A gold banner appears on the judge's tablet. They fix the score, click Review & Submit, and re-lock the card.

🔐 Complete Audit Trail

Every reopen event records the timestamp, who authorized it, and the written reason. No score can ever be changed in secret.

🎙️ Presenter Script: "If a judge makes a typing mistake, they can't just silently edit it. The score desk must unlock it, and the system forces us to type a written reason. Everything is logged in the permanent audit trail."

Reliability & Peace of Mind

What If Something Goes Wrong?

The platform is engineered to handle real-world venue surprises smoothly.

Problem

A judge's iPad loses Wi-Fi

Solution: The tablet automatically switches to the backup 5G cellular hotspot (SHABACH-BACKUP). Any typed scores stay preserved in tablet memory. Nothing is lost.

Problem

A voter didn't get their email code

Solution: Advise them to check their Spam/Promotions folder or use an alternative email. In rehearsal mode, the score desk can view the code instantly.

Problem

Someone tries to vote 10 times

Solution: Blocked automatically by the database. Exactly one ballot per email address is allowed.

Problem

Venue power fluctuation

Solution: All 4 tablets are battery-powered on power banks. The score desk runs on uninterruptible laptops with local storage persistence.

🎙️ Presenter Script: "We have planned for every venue scenario: Wi-Fi drops, power flickers, duplicate votes. The tablets hold battery all day, the app preserves work locally, and the backend prevents any double voting."
